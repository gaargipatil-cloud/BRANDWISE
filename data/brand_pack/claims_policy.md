# Finwise Regulatory and Claims Policy

## Section 1: Accuracy and Source Backing
Every factual claim regarding market returns, savings figures, customer counts, or operational history must match the Verified Fact Sheet exactly.

## Section 2: Prohibited Guarantees
Guarantees of financial return, zero risk, or instant wealth are strictly prohibited. Phrases such as "guaranteed returns", "risk-free investing", or "we guarantee you will double your money" must never be published under any circumstance.

## Section 3: Third-Party Rankings
Ranking claims (such as "#1 finance app", "best budgeting tool", or "top-rated savings tool") need a cited, dated third-party source in the post or email body. Self-declared or uncited superlative titles are forbidden.

## Section 4: Savings Figures and Footnotes
Savings figures must always use qualifying language such as "up to" and carry an explicit footnote or asterisk linking to the underlying calculation (for example: "*Based on a 2023 internal study of checking account fees"). Absolute statements such as "save 30%" without "up to" are non-compliant.

## Section 5: Regulatory Disclaimers
Any text mentioning investing, market portfolios, partner bank deposits, or annual yields must include the mandatory disclosure: "Finwise is a financial technology company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided by partner banks, Members FDIC."
