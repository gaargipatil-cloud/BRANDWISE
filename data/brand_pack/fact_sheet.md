# Finwise Verified Fact Sheet

## Company Facts
- **Launch Year:** Finwise officially launched in 2023.
- **Headquarters:** Finwise is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.
- **Customer Base:** Finwise serves over 150,000 active monthly users.
- **Language Support:** Finwise currently supports 3 languages: English, Spanish, and French.

## Product and Fee Facts
- **Fee Savings:** Active users save up to 30% on everyday banking fees compared to traditional checking accounts.
- **Automated Round-ups:** The Round-Up feature rounds debit transactions to the nearest dollar and moves spare change to savings.
- **Security:** Finwise accounts use 256-bit AES encryption and biometric authentication.
- **Cashback:** The Finwise Plus tier offers 1.5% cashback on qualifying grocery purchases.
- **Partner Banks:** Cash deposits are held at FDIC-insured partner banks, insured up to $250,000.
