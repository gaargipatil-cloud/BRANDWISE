# Finwise Voice and Tone Guide

## 1. Brand Identity
Finwise is a calm, transparent personal finance app designed to help everyday savers make sense of their money. We speak with honesty, clarity, and steady optimism. We treat the user's money with deep respect.

## 2. Core Voice Pillars
- **Direct, never evasive:** Tell users exactly how fees work, what features cost, and what terms apply. Never hide critical conditions behind vague buzzwords.
- **Supportive, never pushy:** Encourage good habits without high-pressure sales tactics, artificial scarcity, or urgent countdowns.
- **Plain English, never Wall Street jargon:** Avoid insider acronyms. Explain financial concepts simply so anyone can take action with confidence.

## 3. Style and Formatting Rules
- Write in active voice and address the reader as "you".
- Keep social posts under 280 characters when writing for short-form social channels.
- Keep headlines under 80 characters for email subject lines and push notifications.
- Do not use sensational punctuation like multiple exclamation marks ("!!!") or all-caps shouting ("FREE MONEY").
