About Brandwise

Brandwise is an editorial review engine providing strict three-state verification (SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, UNVERIFIABLE) against brand facts and compliance standards.

  • Model: Google Gemini (REST endpoint via httpx)
  • Retrieval: scikit-learn TF-IDF passage retrieval over Finwise brand documents
  • Backend: Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite
  • Frontend: Single-file Vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript (Zero build step, zero external runtime JS libraries)

Built with AI assistance (Google AI Studio) during the event.
